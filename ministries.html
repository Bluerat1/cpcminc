OUR SPECIALIZED CALLING

Confined Ministry & Outreach

Bringing the hope of the Gospel beyond the church walls to the lost, least, and confined in Cagayan de Oro City and across Region 10.

Prison & Jail Ministry

CPCM’s prison ministry began in 2002. Between 2006 and 2012, the ministry expanded across different jails in Region 10 and beyond, conducting ongoing outreach and discipleship inside correctional facilities. Through a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology National Headquarters (BJMP NHQ), CPCM workers minister regularly inside jails.

Livelihood & Employment

CPCM has provided livelihood training for prisoners inside jails. In 2012, CPCM partnered with several Christian employers to help ex-PDL (People Deprived of Liberty) members find meaningful employment, helping them rebuild their lives upon release.

Families & Children

Established partly to address the vital needs of ex-PDLs and their loved ones, CPCM’s ministry includes AWANA training and supportive spiritual growth programs for the children of prisoners and local families.

Community Care

CPCM’s holistic care programs have included medical missions, community development projects inside jails (such as water-filtration systems for clean drinking water), and annual Christmas drives for inmates and local communities.
