OUR HERITAGE & VISION

Our Story

CPCM’s ministry grew from a calling to serve people affected by incarceration and bring the transforming power of the Gospel to communities across Cagayan de Oro City and Region 10.

Our Mission

To train leaders and mobilize churches to bring holistic transformation to the lost, least and last people of society through the power of gospel.

Our Vision

To see marginalized people experience holistic transformation through the Power of the gospel and the concerted effort with strategic ministry partners.

FAITHFUL JOURNEY

Ministry Historical Timeline

1998

Rev. Charlemagne G. Delez was released from jail, marking the spiritual seed of what would become CPCM.

2002

Pastor Charlou started prison ministry along with young people from his church, Fortress of Life.

2005

Pastor Charlou recruited Christians from other churches to join in prison ministry. In November 2005, Pastor Charlou opened the first Christian Partnership for the Confined Ministry Inc. (CPCM) church, established to meet the needs of ex-PDLs and their loved ones.

2006 – 2012

The ministry expanded across different jails in Region 10 and beyond.

2012

CPCM partnered with several Christian employers to help CPCM ex-PDL members find employment.

2013

A second church was planted through the initiative of an ex-PDL member.

2021

A third church was planted at Mantibugao, Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon by a CPCM pastor who was also an ex-PDL. During this time, a generous ministry partner donated a church lot and provided allowance support for prison ministry workers.

2022

A generous anonymous donor bought a church lot in Cagayan de Oro City for ex-PDLs and others.

2022

Greenhills Christian Fellowship (GCF) and Union Church of Manila (UCM) played a key role in helping CPCM establish a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology National Headquarters (BJMP NHQ) for national jail access.
