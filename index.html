WHO WE ARE

Born from Prison Ministry, Dedicated to Transforming Lives

Christian Partnership for the Confined Ministry Inc. (CPCM) is an evangelical church birthed out of prison outreach in Cagayan de Oro City. We train Christian leaders, care for prisoners and their families, and equip believers to share God's love with the marginalized.
